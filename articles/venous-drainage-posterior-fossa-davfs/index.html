10 · Microsurgical management — by fistula site

Each sinus,
its own plan.

transverse-sigmoid sinus dAVFs
  • The commonest site — the shunt lies at the transverse-sigmoid junction.
  • Feeders converge from ECA (main), ICA (MHT tentorial branches), and VA systems.
  • Extended retrosigmoid with sinus skeletonization exposes and disconnects the shunt.
marginal sinus dAVFs
  • Foramen magnum / hypoglossal canal region — the spinomedullary junction.
  • Fed by the APA's neuromeningeal branch, the occipital artery, and VA meningeal branches.
  • Far lateral approach, with paracondylar extension when needed.
petrosal sinus dAVFs
  • Cerebellopontine space — superior and inferior petrosal sinuses.
  • Supplied by clival MHT branches, the MMA, the vertebrobasilar system, and the APA.
  • Retrosigmoid, extended retrosigmoid, or retrolabyrinthine corridor.
Concept: transverse-sigmoid fistulas may recruit a large number of meningeal tributaries — the abnormal connection is commonly localized at the borderline between the transverse and sigmoid sinuses.13 / 14
