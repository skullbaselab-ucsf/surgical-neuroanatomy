02 · Anatomical considerations — figure 2

Every foramen
has a tenant.

figure 2 · occipital bone overview↗ enlarge

Name the openings

  • Jugular foramen — CNs IX–XI, the jugular bulb, inferior petrosal sinus.
  • Hypoglossal canal — CN XII, above the anterior third of the condyle.
  • Condylar canal — the posterior condylar emissary vein.
  • Jugular tubercle — the bony obstacle above CN XII that the supracondylar variant removes.
Concept: the hypoglossal canal is the anterior limit of condylar resection — the landmark that protects CN XII.03 / 21

03 · Anatomical considerations — figure 3

C1 is
a ring.

figure 3 · C1 anatomy↗ enlarge

Why C1 matters here

  • The VA runs in the sulcus arteriosus on the posterior arch.
  • Partial resection of the posterior arch extends the exposure inferiorly.
  • The transverse foramen and lateral mass orient the VA's vertical segment.
Concept: the atlas has no body — just two arches and two lateral masses, with the vertebral artery grooving its roof.04 / 21

11 · Step — C1 arch — figure 6

Open the ring
of the atlas.

figure 6 · resection of the posterior arch of C1↗ enlarge

How much C1 is enough?

  • Resect the posterior arch from the midline toward the VA's sulcus.
  • The VA and its venous plexus sit on the arch — free them before the rongeur bites.
  • Arch removal extends the craniotomy to the foramen magnum rim and beyond.
Concept: the shaded portion of the arch is what comes out — the artery rides its roof, so the dissection plane stays strictly subperiosteal.12 / 21

15 · Transcondylar variant — figure 8

The posterior third
of the condyle.

figure 8 · posterior-third condylectomy↗ enlarge

Why only a third?

  • Removing the posterior third of the occipital condyle opens the anterior foramen magnum.
  • The hypoglossal canal — above the anterior third — is the stop sign.
  • More than half the condyle risks atlanto-occipital instability.
Concept: the transcondylar corridor reaches the pontomedullary junction and the anterior margin of the foramen magnum — limited anteriorly by the hypoglossal canal.16 / 21

19 · Modifications — figure 11

Above and beside
the condyle.

figure 11 · supracondylar and paracondylar modifications↗ enlarge

Two more doors

  • Supracondylar: jugular tubercle out → anterior medullary cistern and lower clivus.
  • Paracondylar: jugular process drilled → jugular bulb and the exit of the lower cranial nerves.
Concept: both variants spare the condyle's articular surface — the clivus above, the jugular foramen beside, no instability added.20 / 21
