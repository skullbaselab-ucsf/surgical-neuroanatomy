Craniometric
points

The skull's coordinate system — external landmarks mapped to the cortex, ventricles, and venous sinuses beneath them.

07 · Cortical surface — figure 5

Seven windows,

figure 5 · craniometric points and cortical surface↗ enlarge

The seven windows

  • Superior frontal, inferior frontal, sphenoidal.
  • Superior parietal, inferior parietal.
  • Temporal and occipital.
  • Each frames a named cortical structure under a named surface point.
Concept: the Sylvian fissure's anterior and posterior Sylvian points are the key references for splitting the fissure — and the squamous suture traces its direction.08 / 16

09 · Strategic burr holes — model 7

Four named
entry points.

interactive model 7 · keyholes and ventricular accesses

Name the point, name the target

  • Kocher: 11 cm behind nasion, 1–2 cm anterior to the coronal suture, on the midpupillary line → frontal horn.
  • Keen: 2–3 cm above and behind the pinna → trigone.
  • Frazier: 6 cm above the inion, 3–4 cm off midline → occipital horn.
  • Dandy: 3 cm above the inion, 2 cm lateral → occipital horn.
Concept: Kocher's precoronal point stays lateral to the sagittal sinus and anterior to the motor strip — 6 cm of catheter reaches the frontal horn.10 / 16

12 · Venous anatomy — figure 9

Respect the vein
of Labbé.

figure 9 · Sylvian fissure and vein of Labbé↗ enlarge

Where does Labbé drain?

  • Arises from the middle of the Sylvian fissure, descends to the transverse sinus.
  • Found 0.8–1.5 cm above the zygomatic arch, 2–5 cm behind the external auditory meatus.
  • Joins the tentorial venous group ~7 mm from the superior petrosal sinus.
Concept: Trolard crosses the postcentral sulcus 1.2 cm behind the central sulcus; Labbé anchors the posterior Sylvian region — sacrifice neither.13 / 16

13 · Posterolateral keyhole — figure 10

The asterion
lies.

figure 10 · asterion and transverse-sigmoid junction↗ enlarge

Finding the transverse-sigmoid junction

  • The asterion overlies the transverse sinus more often than the junction itself.
  • Ribas: junction 1 cm anterior to the asterion, burr hole edge at the petromastoid line.
  • Teranishi: burr hole 0.65 cm inferior and 0.65 cm lateral to the asterion.
Concept: junction of lambdoid, parietomastoid, and occipitomastoid sutures — the superior limit of the retrosigmoid craniotomy, and the gateway to the occipital artery below.14 / 16

14 · Anterolateral keyholes — figure 11

One hole,
two cavities.

figure 11 · strategic burr hole positions↗ enlarge

The MacCarty keyhole

  • On the frontosphenoidal suture — 6.8 mm superior and 4.5 mm posterior to the frontozygomatic suture.
  • Reveals frontal dura above and periorbita below.
  • In the pterional approach: 5–6 mm posterior to the three-suture junction.
Concept: sutures, prominences, and points have located craniotomy burr holes since the 19th century — the modern keyhole is a measured point, not a guess.15 / 16
