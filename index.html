About this library

Each entry takes a peer-reviewed article from the UCSF Surgical Neuroanatomy Collection and rebuilds it as a browser-based slide deck: the Sketchfab 3D models are the main assets, supported by the article's figures and source tables. Click any figure in a deck to inspect it full-screen; navigate with the arrow keys.

The decks are plain HTML — they open in any modern browser on any computer. Embedded 3D models stream from Sketchfab, so an internet connection is needed for the interactive models.

About the lab

The UCSF Skull Base & Cerebrovascular laboratory is staffed by world-renowned neurosurgeons and otolaryngologists in the fields of skull base, neuro-oncology, and cerebrovascular surgery. Every year, we welcome talented research fellows who, with their work and commitment, are the heartbeat of the laboratory's daily function.

Under the guidance of director Dr. Roberto Rodriguez Rubio, MD, the Skull Base lab has trained dozens of such researchers, from international surgeons and UCSF residents to medical students and undergraduates.

Since its conception in 2012, the laboratory has produced numerous publications in the most prestigious journals in our field, some of which have redesigned the surgical treatment and approach for specific lesions and provided a sound scientific basis for optimal practices in operative neurosurgery and otolaryngology.
